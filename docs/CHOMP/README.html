CHOMP

The team-preview solver for Champions VGC Reg M-C

Version 3.0.0 · 2026-10-13

CHOMP decides what to do at team preview: which four Pokémon to bring and which two to lead. It reads both open team sheets and returns a mixed strategy over the 90 bring-and-lead options. It scores every pair of options with a learned win chance, then solves the resulting game exactly.

CHOMP v1 is part of ABRA. Its code is in ABRA's solver/chomp/. It plays on the Pokémon Showdown ladder through ABRA's live client, ROTOM. This repository holds the CHOMP documents and the retired browser tool.

Results, with sources and limits: the white paper. In short: CHOMP beats the most common human bring for a team, and it shows no clear edge over the bring a human chose after seeing the opponent.

Run it

See the technical documentation, Part 1. CHOMP runs from an ABRA checkout with ABRA_REGULATION=regmc.

Retired: the browser userscript (Reg M-B)

Until 2.5.1 CHOMP was a Tampermonkey userscript for Reg M-B on Pokémon Showdown. It is retired: it targets a retired regulation and its species data is out of date. The code stays in app/, engine/, build/ and tests/ as history, and CI still runs its tests. Its Reg M-B validation is in docs/VALIDATION-REPORT.md. Do not use it for Reg M-C.
