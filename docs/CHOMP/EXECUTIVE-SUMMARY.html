CHOMP — Executive Summary

One page · Version 3.0.0 · 2026-10-13

The problem

At team preview in competitive Pokémon doubles, each player secretly picks four of six Pokémon to bring and two of those four to lead. Each side has 90 options, and both choose at once. In Champions VGC Reg M-C with open team sheets, both full sheets are visible, so the choice is a small, fully specified game.

The approach

CHOMP scores all 90 × 90 option pairs with a learned chance to win, then solves the game exactly for a mixed strategy that an opponent cannot exploit. In games 2 and 3 of a best-of-three it leans halfway towards punishing the opponent's previous pick. It lives inside ABRA and plays on the Pokémon Showdown ladder through ABRA's client, ROTOM.

Evidence

Every figure, with its source, is in the white paper §3. In brief: in pre-registered simulated tests CHOMP beat the most common human pick for each team, and it showed no clear edge over the pick a human made after seeing the opponent.

Status

CHOMP v1 is live at preview in ABRA's ladder arm. A later version with stat spreads and field effects did not beat v1 and was not promoted. The ladder runs so far are too short to measure CHOMP's effect on rating.

Retired

The old CHOMP browser userscript for Reg M-B (versions 1.0 to 2.5.1) is retired. Its code stays in this repository as history.
